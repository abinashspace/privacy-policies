Privacy policy

Cookie Note · 20 November 2026

Cookie Note lets you write a short note, seal it in a cookie, and share a link. There is no account.

On this phone

The app keeps these on the device, and nowhere else:

  • An optional profile: photo, name, age, and description.
  • Cookies you saved in the Jar, sent or received.
  • Your background colour.
  • The last cookie count this phone has seen.
  • Ad consent choices, and a few preferences used to time ads (whether this is the first launch, and when an interstitial was last shown).

Export writes a file you choose to save. Import reads a file you choose. Uninstall the app, or clear its storage, and this data is gone from the phone.

The link is the note

When you share, the note is inside the link. It is encoded, not encrypted. Anyone with the link can read it. The app does not upload the note to a Cookie Note database, and a shared note cannot be edited or deleted later, because the link is the note.

Opening a link loads cookie-note.pages.dev. The note is in the address, so the site receives that address the way any website receives the page you open.

The cookie count

Cracking a cookie, or sharing a Cookie-tab wish, sends one number to cookie-note.pages.dev/api/count: how many to add, from 1 to 5. The request does not include the note, your name, your profile, or an account id. The site stores a single shared total.

Ads

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the Jar and Profile tabs, an interstitial after you share a cookie, and a rewarded ad if you choose a custom colour or a non-default ink or font.

Google's advertising SDK may collect your advertising ID and other technical information, such as IP address and device data, to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where allowed. How Google processes that information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The app declares the advertising ID permission (AD_ID) so Google can use that identifier, subject to your consent and your device settings.

Consent

Where required, the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change those choices later: open the Profile tab and tap Privacy options, when that row is shown.

This policy is also available in the app, under Profile, and at abinashspace.github.io/privacy-policies/cookie-note/.

What this app does not do

No analytics SDK. No sale of data. The photo picker is the system picker, and Cookie Note does not ask for access to your gallery.

Children

Cookie Note is not directed at children.
